* [Workshop materials](https://digital-methods-sydney.github.io/ws-201812/)

**Role:** Facilitator
**Host:** DH Downunder 2018 Summer School, The University of Sydney · **Date:** December 2018

Professional development workshop on computational text analysis using R, covering
data import from multiple sources (PDFs, HTML, XML, APIs), text tidying with
tidyverse and tidytext, document–term matrices, and Structural Topic Models (STM)
for discovering latent thematic patterns, with hands-on exercises on literary
texts and web-scraped content.
