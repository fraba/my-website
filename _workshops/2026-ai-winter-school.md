**Role:** Lead designer and presenter
**Host:** Centre for AI, Trust and Governance, University of Sydney · **Dates:** 27–30 July 2026 · Camperdown

I led the design of the inaugural AI Winter School, a four-day intensive program
for postgraduate researchers and academics spanning the technical foundations of
AI, its ethical and governance implications, and its use across the research
process. I set the curriculum and session structure across the four thematic days
(AI for research; technical foundations; AI for writing and literature review;
data analysis and modelling), convened the speaker program, and taught two
sessions:

* [What Are Researchers Actually Using AI For?](/6PXD4BVN/) — Day 1
* [AI for Literature Review](/PZ6NTWJG/) — Day 3
