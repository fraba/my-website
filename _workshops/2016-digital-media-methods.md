* [Workshop materials](https://fraba.github.io/digital_media_methods_sydney/)

**Role:** Facilitator
**Host:** Department of Media and Communications, Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences, The University of Sydney · **Dates:** November–December 2016

Two-day professional development workshop introducing R programming fundamentals
and social media data collection. Day 1 covered R/RStudio basics, data
manipulation with dplyr, visualisation with ggplot2, and text mining with the tm
package using Twitter data from the #smachedavo protest. Day 2 focused on API
authentication, live Twitter data collection with the twitteR package, control
flow, and automated data scraping workflows.
