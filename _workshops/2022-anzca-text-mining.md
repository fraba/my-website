* [Workshop materials](https://fraba.github.io/2022-ANZCA-workshop-The-art-of-text-analysis/)

**Role:** Facilitator
**Host:** ANZCA Winter School Training Session, University of Canberra · **Date:** July 2022

Professional development workshop for academic and industry researchers covering
data visualisation with Tableau (including JSON data import and text analysis
functions), an introduction to R and the tidyverse, text mining workflows (import,
tidying, modelling, presentation), document–term matrices, and topic modelling
using Structural Topic Models (STM), with hands-on exercises analysing literary
texts and social media data.
