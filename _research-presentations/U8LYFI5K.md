This panel discussion, hosted by the University of Sydney's Faculty of Science
and moderated by Reged Ahmad (Guardian Australia, *Full Story* podcast),
explored the psychology behind polarisation and misinformation — examining how
biases, group identities and media algorithms shape our beliefs — while
identifying commonalities and possible solutions.

Panelists were Professor Fiona White (School of Psychology, Director of the
SUPIR Lab, specialising in reducing stigma and prejudice), Professor Micah
Goldwater (School of Psychology, focusing on countering misinformation and
improving critical thinking), and Dr Francesco Bailo (School of Social and
Political Sciences, examining political engagement on social media).
