Paper Presentations 3: Governing Artificial Intelligence. The slides, titled *The Tower of Babel Effect: AI Underspheres, Creative Remix, and the Politics of Regulation across Singapore, Vietnam, and Australia*, present a pilot study that follows YouTube recommendation pathways to ask whether, as global AI practices move across borders, governance moves with them.
